description: Check equations, numerical methods, reproducibility, reference comparisons, and claims when changing or reviewing physical simulation models.
---

Read `.physicore/ops/SCIENTIFIC_GATES.md` and the affected feature's spec and tests, not every project document. Identify the decision and valid domain. Re-run the smallest meaningful reproducible checks, inspect tolerance changes, reference provenance, mesh/time sensitivity when applicable, and whether results are consistent with published physical evidence. Produce a feature evidence record showing exact observed commands/numbers and limitations. Label own review as internal review, not independent validation; request qualified human/experimental evidence where necessary.
