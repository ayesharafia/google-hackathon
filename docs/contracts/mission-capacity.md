# Local mission capacity contract

New local studies use `local_mission_capacity_v3`, validated by `drone/local_results.py`. This is a separate local draft-study contract. Existing approved-package `AircraftStudyV1`/`AircraftStudyV2` and sphere `ResultsV1` remain unchanged. A general approved aircraft V3 remains pending under IP10; local consistency validation cannot provide authenticated engineering approval.

Every new study binds its frozen project revision, canonical snapshot hash, retained performance-source byte hashes, model source fingerprint and NumPy/SciPy versions. The canonical result bytes are retained as an artifact. Export returns those same bytes. Repeated submission retains one winning result association; old revisions and studies survive draft edits and restart.

For required alternatives, the headline is the minimum individual bound only after every required case passes again at that same payload. Zero payload is not assumed feasible. There is no bisection or extrapolation across a missing feasible interval.

For a chronological mission, the typed successor supports a lower aggregate bound without inventing a limiting case: `aggregate_limit=sequence_energy`, `limiting_case_id=null`. Each segment names an eligible case, explicit duration, source note and `power_profile=constant`. The joint bounded allocation enforces required cases and the one-pack aggregate energy budget. It independently recomputes all cases at the resulting common payload and retains a segment energy ledger. Missing evidence, differing common payloads, residual failures, altered segment powers or inconsistent energy sums refuse contract validation.

`feasible` identifies a numerically checked point inside the declared restricted model. `infeasible` needs a checked bounded linear-combination certificate. Solver failure, time limit or a failed recomputation is `unresolved`. Missing or uncovered physics/data is `unsupported`. None establishes installed-aircraft validation or an operational payload allowance. All local records have `engineering_approval=pending`.

Certificate bound provenance distinguishes declared physical limits, map coverage, analyst payload domains and fixed candidate queries. A certificate relying on coverage/domain endpoints is retained as `unsupported`, not physical infeasibility. Map-derived power/torque rows may themselves rely on incomplete operating coverage. If admissible RPM states below or above the retained map remain unknown, every certified contradiction is conservatively `unsupported`, even when its coverage-bound multipliers are zero. A failed certificate calculation remains `unresolved`. Sequence-only optional cases use their explicit segment duration for energy/duty, while required standalone cases retain their declared duration. Participating-case bounds establish an attaining case when aggregate energy does not bind.

Local records are immutable observations, including unresolved outcomes. Wall-clock limits can make a fresh execution differ under machine load; the first accepted association wins for repeated submission. This is not a deterministic execution guarantee or a recoverable-attempt protocol. IP15 will provide explicit attempts/retry semantics. Saving/freezing a new draft version currently creates another revision association.

Supported retained local V1/V2 records are read without migrating their bytes in place. Unknown V2 producer fingerprints with incomplete evidence may be refused; arbitrary edited-tree histories are not guaranteed readable. V2 introduced chronological energy. V3 adds mandatory individual bounds for every participating case, consistent aggregate attribution and common-candidate checks. Known historical V2 producer generations dispatch by exact retained source fingerprint: the first `07b9f19` policy and corrected `0db6bb0`/`3f9b01d` policies remain available. Later or unknown V2 producers must satisfy the stronger evidence checks; absence of bounds cannot select a weaker policy. Every historical record has a visible historical qualifier in browser/PDF; reading them does not requalify earlier physics under V3. The specification of each new study also binds its result schema version. An older model fingerprint remains associated with the old study; rerunning changed code creates another specification hash. The fingerprint is a local source/library identity, not an immutable deployment attestation.

The initial profile excludes variable-power trajectories, nonzero inflow, maneuvers, thermal/sag state and battery resizing. Constant-power integration is exact (`P*duration`); it needs no numerical quadrature. Variable-power quadrature and independently reviewed uncertainty scenarios remain separate IP11/IP12 extensions.
