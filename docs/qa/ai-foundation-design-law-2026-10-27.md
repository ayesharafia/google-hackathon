# Interface law review: AI foundation

Reviewed the full owner-supplied law again (source SHA-256 `a9e8d49155afba53e2c4621028a2c7bda679dd09841d77bc9c251441d5248ee7`) against the new workbench panel and captured desktop, phone, enlarged and review states. This is an addition to an existing engineering tool, not a new marketing page; hero, pricing, testimonial, footer, image seam and brand campaign prescriptions are not applicable to this panel.

- **Purpose and cohesion:** the actual saved design, precise before/after values and user-controlled review are the focal artifact. The panel follows the existing green and ink workbench palette, type and controls; no invented clients, logos, fake chat or fake metrics were added. No new font or icon library is introduced.
- **Legibility and spacing:** labels, values and buttons use existing high-contrast ink; panel gutters remain at desktop and phone widths. At 2x CSS enlargement the original two-column form cramped its button; container-based stacking corrected it. Long IDs are shortened in visible summaries while exact records remain available. The comparison table preserves row alignment and scrolls to its units on narrow screens.
- **Motion, backgrounds, depth and effects:** no entrance animation, glows, glass, gradients, grid, decorative shadow, hover lift, underlined hover or clipped silhouettes were introduced. Controls and their content do not depend on animation. No decorative icon tiles or status pills are used.
- **Interaction and state:** real browser actions exercised credential creation, visibility, removal, revocation, discovery, preview, approval, undo, receipt inspection and project switching. Viewer and public-mode restrictions were also exercised. Keyboard order and visible focus were checked; no screen-reader claim is made. Controls are disabled when context or permission makes an action unavailable. Pending reviews remain visible; older receipts are explicitly expandable.
- **Composition:** the design preserves the workbench's functional hierarchy and the existing project context, with a compact form and a real data table rather than a decorative landing-page stack. The panel adds no centered symbols or custom SVG whose optical centering needs a separate check. Button text and rows were inspected at enlarged size. The phone comparison is deliberately scrollable, not clipped.
