## Assessment keys

- **T**: Existing Optima/Candara display and neutral body roles retained; no font download, brand pairing change, decorative emphasis or mono labels. Numeric signs and full strings stay readable with tabular figures.
- **M**: No new glow, gradient, glass, shadow, texture or background layer. Existing green/ink tones and native focus/hover states stay cohesive; text/button contrast inspected in final screenshots.
- **I**: Native selectors, disclosures and same-frozen-case navigation respond to input. Pending/context guards are real. No decorative lift/reveal/underline animation or hidden entrance state; existing reduced-motion and orbit interaction retained.
- **A**: No new logo, icon pack, boxed mark, fake proof or decorative SVG. Existing native disclosure/select marks and real frozen-design viewer remain meaningful; no icon stripping or invented customer assets.
- **G**: One evidence sheet, aligned tables and responsive field/value grids. Context screenshots prove gutters. Whole numeric cells scroll horizontally; residual/interval labels have deliberate minimum widths. No new clip, notch, overlap or forced height.
- **C**: This bounded engineering inspector extends the actual workbench. Its authored choice separates retained operating points and restores the same frozen case. No new landing-page, hero, footer, pricing, island or marketing skeleton; existing product identity retained.
- **P**: One native navigation action plus existing-style selectors/details; no pill, chip, card, CTA pair, pricing or signup preset. No extra border box or decorative status container.
- **R**: Native select/button/details/table primitives fit this existing vanilla-JS app. Accessible names were corrected and actual behavior checked; no component framework or global style dependency added.
- **S**: Terse labels distinguish the two operating points; scope/missing/diagnostic notes carry evidence. Removed visible implementation-path copy and the redundant missing-reason line. No invented claim, quote decoration or em dash in new UI copy.
