## Assessment keys

- **T**: Existing Optima/Candara display roles and neutral body retained. No font download, pairing, wordmark, decorative italic or mono house labels. Readout values use tabular figures with room for units and exact signs/strings.
- **M**: Existing tonal green/ink system; no added gradient, glow, glass, background, grain, boxed material or shadow. Native control states and final text/control contrast inspected in desktop/phone/enlarged screenshots.
- **I**: Native selectors, discrete point clicks, arrow/Home/End/Enter/Space handlers and existing downloads/reuse respond. Guards clear stale views. Immediate linked selection gives purpose; no boop, underline wipe, decorative float or hidden reveal. Existing 3D orbit/reduced-motion behavior retained.
- **A**: No new icon pack, boxed logo, invented proof or filler asset. Existing native disclosure/select marks retain meaning. Data marks are bare points, not icon tiles; no indiscriminate removal of existing icons.
- **G**: One responsive evidence sheet. Wide controls/readout align in two columns; narrow controls stack. Context captures prove gutters. Numeric cells stay whole and scroll to the last column. No new overlap/notch/clip. Fixed SVG height has measured room for ticks, markers, bars and axis label.
- **C**: Extends the real workbench, with retained sample selection as the authored interaction. Source identity/conditions and literal coverage gaps make this specific to rotor evidence. No new hero/footer/marketing composition, reused landing skeleton, fake app or forced atmosphere. Existing product identity remains.
- **P**: Native quantity/sample selects and existing library actions; no pill metadata, card wrapper, CTA pair, pricing/signup preset, ornamental rail or accent bar.
- **R**: Native browser select, table and existing buttons fit this vanilla-JS app. No framework/dependency needed for these mature primitives. Custom data marks provide names, states, roving focus and explicit key handling; table/native selector is the alternative. Native OS popup/screen-reader behavior remains unverified.
- **S**: Terse, load-bearing labels distinguish electrical/shaft watts, declared coverage and original rows. Exact values and source conditions remain available. Synthetic/measured qualifications are explicit; no invented customer claim, decorative quotation or new UI em dash.
- **D**: SVG is an actual plot of retained samples, not decorative illustration. No fitted line or fabricated data. Filled/hollow markers encode interval membership; selection links to exact values/table. Axes and coverage bars carry data meaning. Rounded axis caps, measured marker centers/tick containment and zero-axis behavior are checked; no full-page grid or background art.
