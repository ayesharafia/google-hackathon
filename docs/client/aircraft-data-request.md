# Aircraft payload study: data request

Please provide the approved design inputs below so PhysiCore can calculate supported payload and identify the limiting condition. Geometry alone does not establish thrust or mass. Send current, source-labelled files; leave unknown values marked unknown. A preliminary review can identify gaps before a complete submission.

**Engineering owner: client.** Please identify the responsible engineer and an independent approver/contact in the submission. They own assumptions, validation tolerances and permission to make operational performance claims. The software team implements calculations and retains their provenance.

| Needed | Please provide |
|---|---|
| Assembly | Original component STL/CAD files, explicit units, component roles, transforms, rotor centers/axes, geometry revision and installation/ground-clearance context. |
| Rotor performance | Measured thrust, torque, shaft/electrical power versus RPM/pitch/inflow/density for the exact rotor/motor combination; units, rotation conventions, test conditions, continuous/burst duration, uncertainty, valid ranges and source rights. If using blade models, supply blade stations and eligible airfoil polars. |
| Mass and placement | Structure, propulsion, battery, avionics, other and installed-payload mass items; CG positions, uncertainty and applicable inertia. Give a payload-placement/CG envelope and external structural allowable loads. Include explicit zero items where truly absent. |
| Pack and electrical limits | Pack mass, measured usable capacity/energy, chemistry/configuration, voltage/current/power limits, discharge and temperature limits; motor/ESC continuous versus burst ratings, efficiencies and auxiliary demand. State whether pack size is fixed or variable. |
| Mission and atmosphere | Every required alternative flight case, and chronological segments separately: duration, altitude, air-relative speed/wind, acceleration/attitude/rates where required, temperature/pressure, ground proximity, fault cases and one explicit reserve convention. |
| Acceptance | Intended claim, applicable model/installation envelope, independently reviewed tolerance/uncertainty policy, comparison measurements and the client engineer who will sign off on conclusions. |

The report will state the approved design revision, payload bound, limiting case/component, residuals, input/model coverage and remaining uncertainty. Incomplete or unsupported cases receive a precise reason and withhold the mission headline. Synthetic examples and the sphere verification do not validate your aircraft. Please confirm a private transfer channel and permitted recipients before sending confidential design files.

Prepared for client review; this request has not been sent to anyone by the agent. No recipient/contact or human approval is invented.
