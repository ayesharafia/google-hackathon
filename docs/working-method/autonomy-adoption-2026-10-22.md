## How a broad request works

The parent chat acts as the main orchestrator and spawns specialist subagents
for the current run. The scientific coordinator selects evidence work with the
architect, routes research/specs/checks/build/review to separate roles and returns
numbers and gaps. The main orchestrator owns shared tracker updates, preventing
multiple agents from editing the same module simultaneously. Use the supplied
budget of up to three tasks per prompted run unless the user specifies another.

The supplied plan and role definitions are the operating design authorized by
the user's adoption request. No additional approval round is needed to install
that design or prepare GREEN work. Later decisions use their actual zone.

## Interpretations that keep the rules consistent

1. **Priority and scope:** drone payload/mission remains first. Shared foundation
   preparation supports that domain. Starter conduction/all-domain tasks do not
   authorize thermal or another domain, and existing GCI tooling is inspected
   before proposing a replacement. Industry needs are researched from primary
   sources and tied to this product, not inferred from a generic feature list.
2. **GREEN:** research, documentation, source inventories and exact-math
   diagnostics may proceed. They do not approve physical error bands or claim
   aircraft accuracy. Preserve existing model semantics and evidence identities.
3. **YELLOW exception:** the revised autonomy policy explicitly permits an
   unapproved draft experiment on `provisional/<feature>`. The approved `/build`
   command retains its approval check. Experimental validation references and
   checks are authored and recorded before builder work, with separate roles.
   Experimental checks do not constitute approved physical tolerances. Every new
   dependency is YELLOW even inside an otherwise GREEN tooling task.
4. **RED:** human spec approval, approved-equation/assumption changes, tolerance
   changes and customer-facing validation claims stay pending until explicitly
   decided. AI scientific coordination is not human sign-off. B's merge approval
   cannot supply missing scientist or customer approval. Do not rewrite an
   existing approved tolerance to make a comparison pass.
5. **Inboxes:** silence, an elapsed three days or a default cell never constitutes approval.
   The unresolved item stays held/deferred. Prepare the concrete review packet,
   park it and move to other ready work. At most three distinct specs await the
   scientist; several questions about one spec do not create extra approved specs.
6. **Tests and evidence:** builder restrictions cover assertions, tolerances and
   reference data in `tests/` as well as `validation/`. Separate AI agents give
   role separation, not human or cross-family approval. Report every performed,
   failed, skipped or unavailable suite separately; unavailable native/provider
   campaigns are not green. A new provisional case need not pass initially, but
   it cannot regress existing baseline checks.
7. **Rollback:** restore only the current task's own changes when necessary.
   Do not reset unrelated work or delete data/features/evidence. If clean recovery
   is not possible, record the regression, preserve the work and stop that run.
   Three failed attempts park a task; they do not justify weaker thresholds.
8. **Branches and merges:** keep all branches as B last instructed. GREEN
   repository setup can be published within the current task's authorization.
   Broad future prompts are not standing permission to merge any provisional
   work. YELLOW work requires explicit approval of that concrete merge, while
   RED scientific decisions retain their own authority.
9. **When it runs:** “keep working”, “update this project” and “next” invoke this
   workflow during the prompted run. Adoption creates no recurring automation,
   daemon, background execution, contact authorization or spending authority.
   Scheduling requires a separate explicit request.
10. **Playbook wording:** Busy Mode now expressly supports broad prompts. The
    older “never say keep building” passage is guidance about evidence, not a
    reason to reject a broad prompt. The agent translates it into a bounded task
    and a measurable deliverable.
