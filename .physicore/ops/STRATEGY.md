# Company Strategy — owner-adjustable, agent-maintained with evidence

**Mission:** Bring scientifically credible simulation capabilities into useful, affordable engineering design workflows. Help customers evaluate designs, understand trade-offs, and shorten cycles of low-value iteration; never promise measured savings without data.

**Operating principle:** A small, end-to-end workflow that a real customer can demonstrate and evaluate is worth more than ten unverified features.

**Active domain in THIS repository (OBSERVED):** drone payload/mission software. Source: README.md, src/physimesh/drone/capacity.py and src/physimesh/projects/service.py. No other domain is selected by this installation.

**Customer persona / decision:** the user confirmed saved-design payload/mission assessment with plots/evidence. External customer identity, measured aircraft inputs and acceptance accuracy remain UNKNOWN.
**Primary product wedge (user-directed):** a retained aircraft-design workbench that reports restricted map-based payload bounds and their limitations across required cases/segments. Commercial demand and real-aircraft predictive accuracy remain unvalidated.
**14-day stretch objective:** An honestly scoped, reproducible demonstrator with understandable inputs, outputs, limitations and numerical evidence. This is not a claim of certification, airworthiness, or commercially validated accuracy.

## CEO selection principles
1. Fix a real regression or false scientific claim before adding shiny features.
2. Remove the largest blocker to a credible customer workflow.
3. Prefer source-supported commercial differentiation and technical feasibility to speculative breadth.
4. Increase confidence with reproducible tests and reference evidence before scale and marketing promises.
5. If no ready work exists, derive **up to three** new tasks from inspected code, tested behavior, an existing user commitment, or a sourced industry gap, not imagination.

## Evidence labels
- OBSERVED: shown by code, runnable test, measurement or first-party documentation.
- RESEARCHED: supported by an identified, dated external source.
- INFERRED: plausible conclusion explicitly differentiated from facts.
- PROPOSED: prospective feature, not implemented.
- UNKNOWN: no evidence yet.
