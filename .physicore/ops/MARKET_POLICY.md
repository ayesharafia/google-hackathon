# CEO market intelligence — focused, sourced, infrequent

Research industry practices and alternatives **when prioritizing a major feature, selecting the first customer wedge, or when the market assumptions become stale**. Do not web-search every routine bugfix. If browsing is unavailable, label market research pending, inspect what exists, and continue a safe task.

For a concrete target customer decision, collect:
1. **Customer job**: what decision, who pays, what they use today, switching difficulty, observed vs proposed demand.
2. **Alternatives**: at most three relevant products/workflows, official feature docs or credible public evidence; acknowledge gaps and dated information. Do not invent pricing or claim competitors lack features from absence of public docs.
3. **Technical standards**: identify applicable documents, editions, legal/regulatory scope, and whether publicly accessible; don't invent clause text or imply compliance.
4. **Opportunity**: one differentiated, practical vertical slice with testable inputs/outputs, expected cost/compute, barriers to adoption, validation burden and rough effort.
5. **Decision**: propose no more than three tasks in TASKS.json; include links in task `source_refs` and log findings in `.physicore/ops/RESEARCH_LOG.md`.

Prioritize research from standards bodies, official company documentation, original papers, published benchmarks, and customer interview data. Verify access, use dates, copyright, licenses and suitability before downloading/reusing datasets. Separate documented fact from hypothesis and assumptions. Do not treat marketing numbers as experimentally measured savings.
